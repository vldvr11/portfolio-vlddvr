Cutout — personal project

A personal project: a Chrome extension I built for myself — it cuts objects out of photos in one click with the SAM 2.1 model, right in the browser. Published on the Chrome Web Store.

Direction
Web & Tools
Format
Interactive
Branches Browser extensions
01

Act IApproach

Built a full Chrome Web Store launch kit (RU/EN descriptions, localization, privacy policy), a v1.3.0 manifest (popup, editor, background service worker, Alt+Shift+X hotkey), and a dedicated 'Cutout' design system: dark theme by default with a blue-violet glow, a light theme, 22–26px/14–16px/999px corner radii, a #4f8bff→#8b5cff gradient, and the Onest typeface. Features: one-click selection, Alt-click/box refinement, brush and eraser touch-up, neural edge refinement, multi-object support, and lossless export (only the alpha channel changes).
