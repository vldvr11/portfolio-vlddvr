★ 14 Motion & Social

DK Leading — social media

Social media for the IT agency DK Leading (one-month outsourcing): agency case cards, posts and videos.

Client
DK Leading (dkleading.com)
Direction
Motion & Social
Format
Static · Video · Print
Branches Social graphics
Industry IT & Digital
01

Act ITask

The brief was to present the company's cases (banks, government platforms, etc.) and team on social media honestly — explicitly without fabricated result metrics, as stated directly in the stories brief.

02

Act IIApproach

Built a 537-icon pack (14 categories, 24×24 grid, monochrome via currentColor, searchable SVG catalog), audited a 9-page brand book from 27 verified source files (a 'cloud-diagram' mark in 4 treatments, a Navy/Indigo/Cyan palette plus a separate palette for the STENA product), prepared a company presentation, and a series of 11+ 'DK Leading Cases' stories with 2–3 tech-stack chips and a scale fact instead of a metric for each case, plus 10 filler posts (memes, team intros, process breakdowns).
