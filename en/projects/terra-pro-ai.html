★ 01 AI Fashion Made with AI

Terra Pro — AI fashion photography

A lookbook shoot without a shoot: on-model photos of Terra Pro retail clothing in city locations, generated by AI from photos of the real garments.

Client
THE ONE (Terra Pro)
Direction
AI Fashion
Format
photo
Tools
Magnific · Nano Banana
Branches Lookbooks Campaigns
01

Act ITask

Produce clothing photos and lookbooks for THE ONE without photo shoots: create and maintain the brand’s recurring model characters, cover items that had no proper photos, and bring slow-moving stock back to life with fresh shots for product cards and ads.

02

Act IIApproach

I take a photo of the real garment and describe the shot the way I would brief a photographer: model, pose, location, light, camera. The AI (Google Nano Banana in Magnific) builds the scene, while the garment has to stay accurate — cut, colour, texture and details. Recurring models and a consistent palette of locations — pale stone, colonnades, cafés — keep the series in one style. Frames with errors in hands, faces or the garment itself are discarded.

03

Act IIIResult

Slow-moving items got a fresh presentation, product cards started getting more clicks (higher CTR), and the brand now has its own recognisable models that carry over from series to series.

Menswear

Outerwear and knitwear: leather jackets, puffers, bombers, jumpers and turtlenecks.

Womenswear and kids

Tailored jackets and suits, overshirts and knitwear in bold colours.
