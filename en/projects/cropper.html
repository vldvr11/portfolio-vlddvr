CROPPER & v/cropper

Two serverless web tools: CROPPER for cropping and exporting images with a logo and frame; v/cropper for trimming and compressing video to an exact file size, with a queue, Telegram/Instagram cover generation, and a built-in guide. Built for personal use and used at work at Terra Pro (THE ONE).

Both tools exist in two interface versions — a base one and a 'new design' one. CROPPER has sections for Export, Badge, Margins, and Log. v/cropper offers a video queue, file-size/quality/bitrate control, off-center cropping, a logo overlay across the whole queue, Telegram/Instagram cover generation, H.264 export, and a 10-point built-in guide.
