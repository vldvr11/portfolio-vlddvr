★ 13 Motion & Social

Sandwich & Co — social media

Social media for Sandwich & Co, a sandwich café in Tashkent: posts, stories, promos and reels.

Client
Sandwich & Co
Direction
Motion & Social
Format
Static · Video
Tools
Illustrator · Photoshop · After Effects
Branches Social graphics Reels & Stories
01

Act ITask

Per the client brief, the main problems were low recognition of the 'sandwich' format and no public face for the brand (few seats, no filming planned). The content needed to sell the product itself, without relying on people or on-site shoots.

02

Act IIApproach

A stories content plan built for fully static content with no shoots: the hero is the product, hands and details; nutrition cards, promos and holiday posts.
