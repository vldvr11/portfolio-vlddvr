Sandwich & Co

Brand identity, logobook, menu design, and SMM content for Sandwich & Co, a sandwich café in Tashkent.

Client
Sandwich & Co (sandwichandco.uz)
Direction
Branding & Identity · Motion & Social · Print & Decks
Format
Static · Video · Print
Tools
Illustrator · Photoshop · After Effects
Branches Identity & Brand books Logos Social graphics
Industry Food & Restaurants
01

Act ITask

Per the client brief, the main problems were low recognition of the 'sandwich' format and no public face for the brand (few seats, no filming planned). The content needed to sell the product itself, without relying on people or on-site shoots.

02

Act IIApproach

Built a visual code (dark green / wood / craft textures, a cursive 'SCo' logo, serif headlines), a logobook, a 62×100 cm menu, a 'nutrition card' format (hand with a plate plus calorie/macro stickers), and a stories content plan rebuilt for pure static content with no shoots — the hero of the content is the product, hands and details.
